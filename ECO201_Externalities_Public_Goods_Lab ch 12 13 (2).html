Show the economic logic and formulas

Social cost adds the external harm: MSC = MPC + MEC. With demand P = 100 − Q and private supply P = 20 + Q, the market quantity is Q = 40. An external cost e makes the efficient quantity 40 − e/2; a fee t makes the market quantity 40 − t/2. A fee matching e aligns the quantities. Try the free-rider test

  1. Keep B contributing. What happens to A's payoff if A stops contributing?
  2. Now set B to free ride. Does A personally benefit from contributing?
  3. Which choice is individually best, and which outcome creates greater total net benefit?
Reveal why this resembles the prisoner’s dilemma

Either neighbor gets a better individual payoff by free riding, regardless of what the other does. Thus both free ride (0, 0) even though both contributing (+2, +2) would leave both better off. Coordinated provision or tax-funded contributions can change incentives.
