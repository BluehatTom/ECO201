Explain

Why does monopoly restrict output?

  1. The demand curve is downward-sloping: selling more units requires a lower price.
  2. MR = MC identifies profit-maximizing quantity, not the price.
  3. Read upward from that quantity to the demand curve to find the monopoly price.
  4. Efficient output is where P = MC; monopoly normally produces less and creates deadweight loss.
D: P = 100 − 2Q
MR = 100 − 4Q
MC = 20 + Q

Assumptions: one-price monopoly, linear demand and costs, fixed cost $160. The efficient benchmark uses the same marginal cost curve and ignores externalities.
