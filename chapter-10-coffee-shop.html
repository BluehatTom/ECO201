03 · Explain

Why does profit disappear?

  1. A differentiated product gives each shop downward-sloping demand and limited pricing power.
  2. MR = MC identifies its profit-maximizing quantity; the demand curve gives the corresponding price.
  3. When economic profit is positive, entry shifts an existing firm's demand left. The price and profit that maximize earnings tend to fall.
  4. At the illustrative long-run equilibrium, price = ATC and economic profit is zero, although price remains above MC.

The model isolates entry. Rival prices, advertising, and quality are held fixed. It is an illustration of the textbook long run, not a forecast of a real coffee shop.
