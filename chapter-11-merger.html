How does the HHI work?

HHI = Σ(each firm's percentage market share)²
Merger change: ΔHHI = 2 × share A × share B
  1. Calculate HHI before merging any firms, using percentage shares (0–100).
  2. Combine the selected firms' shares, remove the target, and recalculate HHI.
  3. Under the 2023 U.S. Merger Guidelines, post-merger HHI over 1,800 together with a change over 100 points creates a structural presumption of competitive concern; a merged share over 30% with ΔHHI over 100 is another presumption.
  4. A presumption is rebuttable. Investigating actual competition and case-specific evidence is essential.

This educational simulation uses made-up market shares and the published 2023 federal guidelines. It is not an official assessment or legal advice.
