Why natural monopolies need special attention

  1. A large fixed cost creates falling average total cost over the relevant demand range, so one firm can be cheaper than several duplicates.
  2. Unregulated monopoly sets MR = MC, resulting in less output and a higher price.
  3. Marginal-cost pricing produces efficient output (P = MC), but price is below ATC; the utility needs a subsidy or another way to cover fixed costs.
  4. Average-cost pricing lets the utility break even but still restricts output relative to the efficient benchmark.
Demand: P = 100 − 1.5Q
MC = $20 per unit
Total cost = $600 + $20Q
ATC = $20 + $600/Q

This is a static teaching model. Real rate cases consider investments, rate structures, changing demand, financing, equity, service quality, and incentives.
